Privacy policy

This OAuth app is used only by its owner to access his own Google account.

  • Data accessed: Gmail messages, read-only, requested from the owner's own devices.
  • Use: personal automation, such as reading sign-in codes sent to the owner.
  • Storage: data and tokens stay on the owner's devices; nothing is collected by a server.
  • Sharing: Google user data is never sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
  • Revocation: remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
